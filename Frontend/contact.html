Frequently Asked Questions

Store & Policy Information

Quick answers regarding tile order processes, measurement guidelines, and warehouse stock transfers.

How does the system calculate total tile box requirements?
Our built-in tile box calculator multiplies your room Length x Width to get the net square footage, adds a user-selected cutting waste buffer (5% to 15%), and divides by the coverage capacity of your chosen tile size to output exact box numbers.
Can stock be transferred between central warehouses and store outlets?
Yes! The Admin inventory module tracks unique Item IDs across multiple outlets and warehouses. Admins can initiate stock transfers between locations to satisfy customer purchase orders.
What is the difference between Floor, Washroom, and Front Elevation tiles?
Floor tiles are high PEI-rated polished/porcelain tiles built for heavy foot traffic. Washroom tiles feature anti-skid water-resistant textures for floors and glossy glazed walls. Front Elevation tiles are weather-resistant 3D textured tiles designed for exterior facades.
